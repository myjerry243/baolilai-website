Liquid line · PVDF fluorocarbon

For façades that must not change.

PVDF is the liquid fluorocarbon system behind most of the world’s high-end aluminium architecture. Baked at 232 °C, built in two to four coats, and specified by the AAMA 2605 class. It is the other half of what Polyshine supplies — the powder range gets you 95% of the way; PVDF is for the façade that is the brand.

Four process rules

Metallic and pearl always need a clear coat

Aluminium pigment oxidises under acid rain and industrial atmosphere. Without a clear coat the face loses brightness before the warranty period ends.

Pearl colour shifts with film thickness

Pearl pigments change hue as the film gets thicker. Uniform film build, and a primer tinted to match the top coat, are the only way to hold colour across a façade.

Orange, red and yellow need protection

The inorganic pigments behind these colours weather faster. A clear coat is recommended even on solid colours.

Film build is a performance parameter

Below the specified thickness the anti-contamination and salt-spray behaviour drop off. Measure on the part, not on the test panel.
