Signature technology

Bonded, not blended.

A dry-blended metallic separates in the box. By the time it reaches the gun the pigment-to-base ratio has drifted, and the part comes out striped.

We bond the metallic pigment to the base particle. The ratio stays fixed through transport, spraying and reclaim — which is why a bonded finish can be reclaimed without a colour shift.
