Certification status

What we hold — and what we do not.

Held：quality management system certification, high-tech enterprise (2025), tax credit rating A.

ISO 9001 — quality management system certificate held.

AAMA 2603 / 2604 / 2605 · QUALICOAT I / II / III · GSB · UL — no product certificate is held. We do not display those marks anywhere on this site, in either language.

What we do have：third-party test reports from the National Building Materials Test Centre covering the AAMA 2605 test items — gloss, adhesion, impact, 4000 h salt spray, 10-year South Florida exposure — plus an in-house batch report with every delivery.

How to use this commercially：a test report usually satisfies a fabricator or a contractor; a certificate is required when the specifier names one. If a tender demands the certificate, tell us early — we will say we do not have it rather than lose the project at the document check.

We test to those methods and can supply third-party reports. If your project requires the certificate itself, we will say plainly that we do not have it and quote the certification route separately. Buyers check — an implied claim is the fastest way to lose an account.

Documents on request: TDS, SDS, batch test report, retained sample, non-hazardous declaration.
