Specification notes

Three things buyers get wrong.

Functional additives change the cure window

Conductive and 9H systems are not drop-in replacements: pigment loading and additive level shift the flow and the cure. Re-run the line trial, do not assume the old oven profile works.

Ask for the test, not the adjective

“Antibacterial” means nothing without a standard and a log-reduction figure. “Conductive” means nothing without a surface-resistance range and a method.

Check compatibility with the pre-treatment

Self-cleaning and conductive films are sensitive to substrate residues. Where the substrate is galvanised or chromated, send a panel before committing to volume.

Send the requirement, not the product name.
