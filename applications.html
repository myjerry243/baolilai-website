Applications

The finish is the last 80 microns of a long process.

Most coating failures we get called about are not powder failures. They are pre-treatment, grounding, oven profile or substrate problems that only show up at the coating stage.

01 · Profiles, façade panels, window systems

Architectural aluminium

Exterior polyester, sand texture and super-durable matte. The critical variables are pre-treatment, film build on complex profiles and colour control across batches.

Recommended：BL-A201 · BL-A202 · BL-M102

02 · Where colour accuracy is the sale

Doors & entrance systems

Entrance doors are sold on appearance, which makes ΔE, gloss and texture repeatability commercial issues rather than technical footnotes.

Recommended：BL-M102 · BL-A202 · BL-M103

03 · Hardness, detergent, line speed

Appliances

White goods need 2H hardness, detergent resistance and a cure window that matches a fast line. Year-round colour consistency matters more than any single test.

Recommended：BL-A201 · BL-I401

RAL 7035
04 · Cost per square metre, not per kilo

Shelving & enclosures

For high-volume, low-margin parts the decision is coverage and first-pass transfer efficiency. A sand texture that covers a scratched substrate beats a cheaper powder that needs rework.

Recommended：BL-A201 · BL-I401

RAL 7035
05 · 130 °C changes the economics

MDF & heat-sensitive parts

MDF, composites and assemblies with plastic inserts cannot survive a 190 °C oven. Low-temperature cure removes charring, edge sanding and part of the energy bill.

Recommended：BL-L301

RAL 9010
06 · Corrosion first

Pipes, valves & steel

Epoxy at 100 µm plus a verified pre-treatment is still the most cost-effective corrosion barrier for pipe, valve and tank work.

Recommended：BL-I401

RAL 7035
