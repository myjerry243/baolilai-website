Using it in a tender

Three things a data sheet decides.

Fix the acceptance values

The sheet gives typical values. The purchase specification fixes the acceptance values — write them from the trial report, not from this page, so nobody argues at inspection.

Prove the method

Each value carries an ISO or ASTM method. A tender that names a method can be answered with a report; a tender that names a certificate cannot — ask us early and we will say which one we can do.

Name the substrate and the pre-treatment

Salt spray and adhesion numbers in this sheet assume a conversion-coated substrate. Quoting the powder without the pre-treatment line is how a 1,000 h claim becomes a 300 h argument.

What is not here

Three documents we do not publish.

Certificates

No AAMA 2603/2604/2605, QUALICOAT, GSB or UL product certificate. We test to those methods and hold third-party reports; the certificate itself we do not have.

SDS

Issued per product and per colour, not published as a library — ask for the one that matches the material on your order, and it will carry that batch.

A warranty

Design life on the durability page is a target tied to test data, pre-treatment and film build. It is not a warranty on its own, and we do not write one into a TDS.

Not on the list
