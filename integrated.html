Integrated surface finishing

Chemistry, powder and liquid — one responsibility.

Most coating failures are born one step before the coating. Baolilai is the powder division of Polyshine Technology, which also supplies the pre-treatment chemistry and the liquid PVDF line. That means one supplier answers for the whole chain instead of three suppliers pointing at each other.

Pre-treatment

The seven steps that decide adhesion.

#StepPurpose
1LoadingHang parts so every surface is exposed and no liquid traps
2Pre-degreaseRemove the bulk of oil and loose soil
3Main degreaseRemove all residual oil — this decides adhesion
4Rinse 1 + 2Two-stage rinse so cleaner is not carried forward
5Silane / zirconium conversionChrome-free, phosphate-free nano ceramic film
6Rinse 3 + DI rinseDeionised final rinse — prevents craters and specks
7Dry-offDry before powder; moisture causes pinholes

Silane / zirconium conversion replaces traditional phosphating: no heavy metals, less sludge, and a film that powder adheres to reliably.

Anodising line sequence: loading → degrease → alkali etch → neutralise → (polish) → anodise → colour → seal. We supply the chemistry for each bath.

#Anodising stepFunction
1LoadingAluminium or titanium jigs, good electrical contact
2DegreaseRemove oil and handling soil
3Alkali etchRemove natural oxide, uniform matte surface
4Neutralise / desmutRemove the black smut left by the caustic bath
5Polish (optional)Chemical or electrolytic brightening

Why one supplier

Compatibility is not a document.

A pre-treatment chemical and a powder are developed against each other. When the passivation film and the powder chemistry come from the same house, the adhesion and salt-spray numbers are repeatable instead of negotiated.

It also shortens the debugging loop: if a batch fails adhesion, there is one电话 to make instead of a three-way meeting.
