Company

A coating plant built around three process problems.

Guangdong Baolilai Industrial Co., Ltd. was founded in 2019 in Sanshui, Foshan — inside the Pearl River Delta metalworking cluster, within two hours of most of the aluminium, appliance and hardware plants we serve.

The plant was built to solve three specific problems: metallic effects that separate, substrates that cannot take a 190 °C oven, and particle size distributions that drift. Each became a patented process system.

The group behind the powder

Polyshine: three lines, one surface.

Baolilai is the powder coatings division of Polyshine Technology Limited (宝利鑫科技). The group also formulates the pre-treatment chemistry and runs a liquid PVDF / water-based industrial coatings line — which is why a coating problem can be solved one step upstream instead of argued about.

R&D centre

Formulation, application testing and custom development under one roof.

CMF laboratory

Colour, Material, Finish: sample boards, gloss and texture development before a line trial.

Digital factory

IoT and production data across the plant: batch traceability, energy and yield monitoring.

Held: ISO 9001 quality management system, high-tech enterprise (2025), tax credit rating A, seven patents. Not held: AAMA, QUALICOAT, GSB or UL product certification — we supply test reports instead.

Where we are going

Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Africa, Central America.

The markets we serve now are the ones that were told a coating could not be both affordable and durable. That is the same problem the group was built to solve at home, and it is why the export line exists.
